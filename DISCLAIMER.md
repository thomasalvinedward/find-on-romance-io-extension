## How the Extension Interacts With Websites

Find on Romance.io reads visible book metadata and, where available, structured metadata from supported book pages. It uses that information to build a Romance.io search query. On Romance.io search-result pages, it may compare available results against the detected title and author and redirect to a book page when the match passes the extension's confidence checks.

The extension does not control, operate, modify, or maintain any supported third-party website. It does not submit orders, make purchases, create accounts, post reviews, alter retailer data, or act on behalf of a retailer or Romance.io.

The selected-text context-menu feature works separately. When a user highlights text and chooses the extension's search command, the selected text is used to open a Romance.io search.
